How to get your data

1
Open Instagram → Settings → Your activity → Download your information
2
Select JSON format, choose Followers and following, request download
3
Instagram emails you a .zip file — drop it below to save a snapshot
4
Repeat in a few days/weeks and drop the new zip — the tool shows who unfollowed
